Tutorial E: Saddle Bracket

Book

Shih, Parametric Modeling with Siemens NX, ch. 4
You learn

Datum CSYS first (BORN), Symmetric extrude, Project Curve, renaming in the Part Navigator, Edit Parameters, Assign Material, Measure Bodies
Sizes

The text gives 63.5, 12.7, 50.8, Ø19.05 and R6.35 mm (2.5, 0.5, 2.0, Ø0.75 and R0.25 in). The L profile is in figures only, so it is this project’s own: 76.2 mm long, a 12.7 mm base, a 19.05 × 50.8 mm upright, a 25.4 × 19.05 mm notch.

In short

  1. Datum CSYS first; FreeCAD asks whether to continue without an attachment; a Body has its own origin anyway.

  2. The L profile, extruded Symmetric 63.5 mm (the value is the total width, as in NX since NX 1926).

  3. A circular end tied to a projected edge.

  4. F2 in the Part Navigator to name the features.

  5. Three design changes: the width 63.5 → 50.8 mm, Until Next → Through All, and a fillet added to the first sketch.

  6. Render › Material › Assign Material: steel. Analysis › Measure › Measure Bodies: 86,504.5 mm³, 7900 kg/m³, 0.6834 kg.

What differs from NX

  • Design changes reopen the feature’s dialog (Edit Parameters) instead of editing a dimension shown in the graphics window.

  • Assign Material applies at once and closes with Close; FreeCAD 1.1 also gives the body the material’s colour.

  • FreeCAD’s own Measure has no mass; Measure Bodies is NX-Style’s.
