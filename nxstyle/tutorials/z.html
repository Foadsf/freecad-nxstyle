Tutorial Z: MBD exchange through STEP AP242

Books

Drake, Dimensioning and Tolerancing Handbook, 5.9.7 (degrees of freedom of a datum reference frame); the plate is ours
Standards

ISO 10303-242 (STEP AP242: semantic PMI), ISO 5459 (datums), ISO 1101 (geometrical tolerances), ISO 286
You learn

the Inspection Plan’s datum reference frames, STEP with PMI, Import STEP with PMI, planning an inspection from the file alone
Sizes

A plate 120 × 80 × 10 with four Ø8 holes

Model-based definition means the model carries the tolerances, and they travel with it as data. Here they go out to a supplier in a STEP file and come back into an empty part as PMI, ready for the inspection plan.

Step by step

1. The plate and its PMI

The plate and its PMI are made by script, as tutorials T and Y make them with the ribbon: datums A (underside), B (left side), C (front side), the hole Ø8 H11 positioned Ø0.2 Ⓜ to A|B|C, side B perpendicular 0.05 to A, the thickness 10 ±0.1.

2. The datum reference frames

PMI › Inspection Plan. Below the characteristics:

A | B | C arrests 6 of 6 degrees of freedom (A 3, B 2, C 1)
A arrests 3 of 6 degrees of freedom (A 3); free: translation along X; translation along Y; rotation about Z

The position needs all six; the perpendicularity of B needs only A.

3. Out: STEP with PMI

PMI › STEP with PMI, a file name, OK: datums A, B and C, two datum systems, the size with H11 and the thickness with ±0.1, the position and the perpendicularity.

4. In: Import STEP with PMI

Ctrl+N, PMI › Import STEP with PMI, the file, OK. The plate comes in with seven PMI, the same as were written, on the same faces.

5. The supplier’s inspection plan

Inspection Plan on the imported part: the same characteristics, limits and datum reference frames as at the source.

Z2

Inspection Plan: the datum reference frames, degrees of freedom arrested (Drake 5.9.7)

ribbon 'PMI › Inspection > Inspection Plan'

differs

Datum reference frames (degrees of freedom, Drake 5.9.7: a plane arrests 3, 2 or 1 in order of precedence, an axis up to 4): / A | B | C arrests 6 of 6 degrees of freedom (A 3, B 2, C 1) / A arrests 3 of 6 degrees of freedom (A 3); free: translation along X; translation along Y; rotation about Z. NX has no such check; a position to A|B|C fixes all six, the perpendicularity to A alone leaves the plate free to slide and turn on A, which is all it needs

ribbon 'PMI › Exchange > STEP with PMI', file name typed, MB2 = OK

What differs from NX

  • NX exchanges PMI through STEP AP242 and JT, with graphical PMI and saved views; NX-Style’s files carry the semantic PMI only.

  • A count (4X), the envelope Ⓔ and the ISO 2768 note have no AP242 form in OpenCASCADE, so this plate is dimensioned without them.

  • The report of the degrees of freedom a datum system arrests is NX-Style’s.

  • The file carries the shape and its PMI; tutorial AL sends the history, the sketch constraints and the parameters too.
