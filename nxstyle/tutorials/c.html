Tutorial C: Rod-Guide

Book

Shih, Parametric Modeling with Siemens NX, ch. 9
You learn

Datum planes (At Angle, offset), Hole on a datum plane, Edge Blend
Sizes

A 76.2 × 50.8 × 19.05 mm block (the book’s 3.00 × 2.00 × 0.75 in) with four holes. The book’s sizes appear only in figures; these are NotebookLM’s reading of them.

In short

  1. The block by Extrude; four holes; Edge Blend on the corners.

  2. Feature › Datum › Datum Plane at an angle to a face, then one offset from it.

  3. Select the offset datum plane, click Hole: a sketch opens on the plane; place the centre, Ctrl+Q, and the Hole dialog opens.

The angled upper block (C9) is skipped: its profile is in a figure only.

What differs from NX

  • FreeCAD’s At Angle plane turns about the face’s own axes in the attachment offset; NX lets you pick the hinge axis.

  • Hole drills at circle centres in a sketch; NX places hole points directly.
