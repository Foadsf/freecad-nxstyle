Tutorial K: Parameter-driven flat bar

Book

Leake and Borgerson, Engineering Design Graphics, ch. 7, Fig. 7-30
You learn

The Expressions table (Ctrl+E), dimensions tied to names, configurations
Sizes

L, N and D for two configurations (A: 1219.2 mm, 5 holes, 50.8 mm; B: 914.4 mm, 8 holes, 25.4 mm; the book’s 48, 5, 2 and 36, 8, 1 in). Width, thickness and hole size are not given: 50.8 × 12.7 mm and Ø12.7 here.

In short

  1. Ctrl+E: type L, N_holes, D and hole_spacing = (L - 2*D) / (N_holes - 1) into the Expressions sheet.

  2. Sketch the bar; its length is =Expressions.L.

  3. The first hole at D from the end; Pattern Feature with N_holes occurrences, hole_spacing apart.

  4. Type configuration B’s values into the same sheet: the bar follows.

What differs from NX

  • NX lists expressions in a dialog; FreeCAD keeps them in a spreadsheet. A dimension refers to a cell as =Expressions.L.

  • FreeCAD refuses names that are unit symbols: the book’s N (the newton) becomes N_holes.
