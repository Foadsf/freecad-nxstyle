Tutorial J: Parametric constraints and relations

Book

Shih, Parametric Modeling with Siemens NX, ch. 5
You learn

Geometric constraints, degrees of freedom, Fix and Relax, dimension names and formulas
Sizes

An 88.9 mm (3.5 in) plate. The triangle’s own sizes are in figures only.

In short

  1. A triangle; Perpendicular, a length and an angle; Fully Fixed; undo and Coincident instead; location dimensions; Relax Constraint on Perpendicular; Vertical; a tangent circle.

  2. At every step, FreeCAD’s Under-constrained: N Degrees of Freedom or Fully constrained is compared with NX’s status line. It matches the book’s state each time.

  3. Extrude the plate.

  4. Name the dimensions and drive them with the book’s formulas after =, for example =Constraints.width / 5 * 1.6; the hole is a third of the height.

  5. Two size changes, 25 × 15 and 25 × 20; the hole follows.

What differs from NX

  • NX numbers dimensions by itself (p0, p1 …); FreeCAD names a dimension in its properties.

  • FreeCAD’s panel says the constraint state where NX’s status line does.
