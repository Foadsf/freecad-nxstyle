Tutorial A: The Adjuster

Book

Shih, Parametric Modeling with Siemens NX, ch. 2
You learn

New part, Extrude from a sketch, Profile, Rapid Dimension, Extrude (Subtract)
Sizes

A 63.5 × 63.5 mm L outline with 19.05 mm legs, extruded 63.5 mm; a 25.4 mm wide, 19.05 mm deep slot (the book’s 2.5, 0.75 and 1.00 in)

In short

  1. Ctrl+N, then X with nothing selected; pick XY_Plane, MB2.

  2. Z (Profile): six MB1 clicks for the L, MB2 to end it.

  3. D: the widths, heights and leg thicknesses.

  4. Ctrl+Q: the Extrude dialog opens; 63.5 mm, MB2.

  5. Select the top face, Home › Feature › Extrude (Subtract); R, two clicks, MB2; dimension the slot; Ctrl+Q; 19.05 mm, MB2.

The step list came from the book’s chapter by way of NotebookLM; its slot placement was ambiguous, so the slot is cut into the top of the vertical leg and the part stays one solid.

What differs from NX

  • Units are a preference, not a choice in the New dialog; NX-Style parts are in millimetres.

  • No dynamic dimensions while you draw and no "scale the whole sketch?" prompt.

  • Subtracting is its own button, Extrude (Subtract); Extrude has no Boolean option.
