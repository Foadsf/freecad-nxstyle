Tutorial AZ: Arbor Press Rack Drawing

Source

Ming C. Leu, Wenjin Tao, Amir Ghazanfari and Krishna Kolan, NX 12 for Engineering Design (Missouri University of Science and Technology), sections 5.2 (creating a drawing), 5.3 (dimensioning) and 5.4 (sectional view).
You learn

New Sheet (size, scale, projection), Base View, Projected View, dimensions, Section View
Part

tutorial AT's rack, Arborpress_rack. The replay runs AT first.
Sizes

the book’s, in inches, typed in millimetres: the drawing’s dimensions read 25.4 times the book’s numbers.

Step by step

1. The sheet

Open Arborpress_rack and press Ctrl+Shift+D for Drafting. Click New Sheet. In the sheet’s properties, choose the template ANSIB_Landscape.svg (B, 11 x 17 in), the scale 0.04 (1:25) and Third angle.

2. The views

Select the rack’s body and click Base View. Set the view’s direction to Front and press MB2 to place it.

Select the front view and click Projected View. Click Above and Right, then press MB2. The top view and the right side view appear lined up with the front view, at its scale. The right view shows the hole in the end of the rack as a circle.

The book now turns off Display Borders. A FreeCAD sheet starts with Show Frames off, so there is nothing to press.

3. Dimensions

Pick the two end vertices of each length in the view and click Horizontal or Vertical:

4. The section

Select the front view and click Section View. Click the Up direction and press MB2. TechDraw cuts through the middle of the view, at the height of the hole’s axis. Move the section below the front view.

Save the part with Ctrl+S; the drawing is saved in it.

Every check reads the drawing back from TechDraw and compares it with the model. The views' edges must span what the Part API gives for each direction. Each dimension must equal the length the Part API’s tooth space and bar give, and the book’s number times 25.4. The section must show the hole’s walls one diameter apart, which happens only when the cut goes through the hole’s axis.

What differs from NX

  • NX asks for the sheet size, scale and projection in one Sheet dialog. In FreeCAD they are the sheet’s properties.

  • NX lists the model views in the Base View dialog. A TechDraw view takes its direction as a property.

  • NX places a projected view where you click round the parent. Here you click a side button. In NX a projected view follows its parent when the parent is dragged; here it stays where it was placed.

  • NX shows a border round each view until Display Borders is unchecked. A FreeCAD sheet starts with Show Frames off, and FreeCAD’s Turn View Frames On/Off stays greyed in 1.1.4. The replay reports the property; it did not measure how the sheet looks.

  • NX infers horizontal or vertical from where you move the pointer. Here you choose the Horizontal or Vertical button.

  • TechDraw dimensions attach to vertices. Where NX snaps to any point on an edge, the replay adds a cosmetic vertex first.

  • The book’s text puts the 240 on the front view, but its figures put it on the top view. The replay follows the figures.

  • TechDraw puts a new section view on top of its base view. You move it afterwards.
