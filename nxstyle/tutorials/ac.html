Tutorial AC: Title block and revision block from the release

Books

Drake, Dimensioning and Tolerancing Handbook, ch. 4 (title block, revision block); Wünsch, Pilz and Vajna, Siemens NX für Einsteiger, 6.3 (Titelfeld ausfüllen)
Standards

ISO 7200 (title block fields), ISO 5457 (sheet sizes and frame)
You learn

Drafting › Populate Title Block, the revision block from the release history
Runs first

T (the guide bush and its drawing) and V (releases A and B)

The guide bush was released as BUSH-100 revision A, then changed (ECO-001: the bore became F7) and released as revision B. Its drawing should say so, without anyone retyping the PLM record.

Step by step

1. The drawing

Ctrl+Shift+D switches to Drafting, and the sheet from tutorial T is selected. It uses FreeCAD’s default template, which has no title block.

2. Populate Title Block

Home › Drawing › Populate Title Block. The sheet has no title block, so the dialog gives it the A4 ISO 5457 template with an ISO 7200 title block. The fields come filled:

  • drawing number BUSH-100 and revision B, from release B;

  • the title Guide bush, which is what release A called the part;

  • the status Released, who released it and the date;

  • the general tolerances from the PMI: ISO 2768-mK. The tolerancing note that Inherit PMI put on the sheet in tutorial T goes, because the title block states it now;

  • the scale and the sheet, from the drawing.

Revision block is ticked: one row for A and one for B, each with what changed.

What differs from NX

  • NX fills the title block from Teamcenter attributes such as DB_PART_NO and DB_PART_REV; here they come from Strata’s release record, or from the same attributes in the document’s Meta.

  • NX’s revision table is a tabular note that you fill in. NX-Style draws it from the releases.

  • Zones (the revision’s place on the sheet grid) are not recorded.
