Tutorials

Tutorials A to N are exercises from NX, GD&T and engineering-graphics textbooks, done in NX-Style the way the book tells an NX user to work. Tutorials O to R are Grasshopper video tutorials done on the Algorithmic Modeling canvas (Katydid). Tutorials S to AC are precision-engineering work: a tolerance stack-up checked against the book’s numbers, a part documented to ISO GPS in 3D, volume claims and assembly clearance in a system layout, a release with an engineering change, a sheet metal bracket whose flat pattern is checked by hand, an opto-mechanical pin location, PMI organised in model views, the model with its PMI exchanged through STEP AP242, a composite position frame evaluated from CMM data, the measurement capability of the inspection methods, and a drawing whose title block comes from the release. Tutorial AD joins a laser-cut tray with tabs and slots and lays its parts flat for the cutter. Every page lists the original steps beside the NX-Style steps, so you can follow along with the book or the video, or without them.

The step tables are not written by hand. The tutorial replay presses the same keys, clicks the same NX-named ribbon buttons, clicks sketch points with MB1 and accepts dialogs with MB2, places canvas components by typing their names and drags wires between grips, then checks each result by its volume. The tables show its last runs on FreeCAD 1.1.4: 429 steps.

Before you start

  • Install and start NX-Style (Installation), and try Your first part in five minutes first.

  • Parts are metric. The books work in inches; their sizes are typed in millimetres (2.5 in = 63.5 mm).

  • Where a book gives a size only in a figure, the tutorial page says so and gives the size used here.

Result Means

same

The book’s NX steps work as written.

differs

It works, but the FreeCAD way is different; the notes say how.

limit

The book’s way fails in FreeCAD itself; a workaround follows in the next steps.

missing

FreeCAD has no such function; a stand-in follows.

skipped

Not replayed; the notes give the reason.

FAILED

Did not work.

The books

  • Randy H. Shih, Parametric Modeling with Siemens NX (SDC Publications): tutorials A, C–G, I–N and W (ch. 13, sheet metal).

  • Gene R. Cogorno, Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing for Mechanical Design, 3rd ed. (McGraw-Hill): tutorial H.

  • James Leake and Jacob Borgerson, Engineering Design Graphics (Wiley): tutorial K.

  • Tutorial B is a synthesised first-part exercise.

  • Bryan R. Fischer, Mechanical Tolerance Stackup and Analysis, 2nd ed. (Taylor & Francis): tutorial S, and the stack-up method.

  • Andreas Wünsch, Fabian Pilz and Sándor Vajna, Siemens NX für Einsteiger – kurz und bündig, 4th ed., section 6.10: the PMI workflow of tutorial T and the model views of tutorial Y.

  • Sham Tickoo, Siemens NX 12.0 for Designers, ch. 12: the default model views, tutorial Y.

  • Paul J. Drake, Dimensioning and Tolerancing Handbook (McGraw-Hill), ch. 6 (ASME and ISO compared), ch. 9 (statistical tolerancing) and ch. 24 (pinned interfaces, tutorial X); Gene R. Cogorno (above), ch. 3, 7, 8 and 13: the checks, the fastener formulas and the gauge sizes.

  • Fischer (above), ch. 15: the lever of an angle error, tutorial X.

  • Cogorno (above), ch. 12 and 13: composite position and its graphic analysis, tutorial AA. Drake (above), ch. 20 and 25: measurement systems and the gauge study example, tutorial AB; ch. 4: the title and revision blocks, tutorial AC.

  • The PLM books of John Stark, Michael Grieves, and Antti Saaksvuori and Anselmi Immonen: release and engineering change, tutorial V.

  • Tutorial U’s layout is ours; Assembly Clearance follows NX’s.

  • ISO values (ISO 286 limits, ISO 2768 general tolerances) were checked against two published tables each; the unit tests also compare them with the ISO 286-1 formulas.

The videos

  • Rhino Grasshopper, Rhino Grasshopper Tutorial (Beginner), the planter: tutorial Q.
