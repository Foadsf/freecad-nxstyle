Tutorials

Build a part, try an engineering workflow, or explore parametric design. The tutorials pair textbook and practical exercises with NX-Style steps and recorded replay outcomes. They explain what works the same way, what differs and where FreeCAD reaches a limit.

  • Start with solid modeling
    The Adjuster for a first sketch and extrude, One Extrude, every way for the command workflow, or the Arbor Press Plate and Impeller Shaft for primitives, datums and holes.

  • Develop your sketching skills
    Try the Arbor Press Base, Impeller blade and Holder for coordinates, tangent arcs and copied curves.

  • Document and inspect a part
    Follow the guide bush to ISO GPS, composite position evaluation and measurement capability exercises.

  • Work with assemblies and fabrication
    Make the Shaft Support assembly, check volume claims and clearance, then try a sheet-metal bracket or laser-cut tray.

  • Explore algorithmic design
    Start with Katydid basics, then build a waffle canopy, faceted planter or attractor-driven frames.

  • Exchange and release the design
    Send design intent through STEP, inspect incoming STEP, follow a release and engineering change, or hand the release to manufacturing in Carbon.

How to read the results

The step tables are not written by hand. The tutorial replay presses the same keys, clicks the same NX-named ribbon buttons, clicks sketch points with MB1 and accepts dialogs with MB2, places canvas components by typing their names and drags wires between grips, then checks each result by its volume. The tables show its last runs on FreeCAD 1.1.4: 744 steps.

Before you start

  • Install and start NX-Style (Installation), and try Your first part in five minutes first.

  • Parts are metric. The books work in inches; their sizes are typed in millimetres (2.5 in = 63.5 mm).

  • Where a book gives a size only in a figure, the tutorial page says so and gives the size used here.

Result Means

same

The book’s NX steps work as written.

differs

It works, but the FreeCAD way is different; the notes say how.

limit

The book’s way fails in FreeCAD itself; a workaround follows in the next steps.

missing

FreeCAD has no such function; a stand-in follows.

skipped

Not replayed; the notes give the reason.

FAILED

Did not work.

The books

  • Randy H. Shih, Parametric Modeling with Siemens NX (SDC Publications): tutorials A, C–G, I–N and W (ch. 13, sheet metal).

  • Gene R. Cogorno, Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing for Mechanical Design, 3rd ed. (McGraw-Hill): tutorial H.

  • James Leake and Jacob Borgerson, Engineering Design Graphics (Wiley): tutorial K.

  • Tutorial B is a synthesised first-part exercise.

  • Bryan R. Fischer, Mechanical Tolerance Stackup and Analysis, 2nd ed. (Taylor & Francis): tutorial S, and the stack-up method.

  • Andreas Wünsch, Fabian Pilz and Sándor Vajna, Siemens NX für Einsteiger – kurz und bündig, 4th ed., section 6.10: the PMI workflow of tutorial T and the model views of tutorial Y.

  • Sham Tickoo, Siemens NX 12.0 for Designers, ch. 12: the default model views, tutorial Y.

  • Paul J. Drake, Dimensioning and Tolerancing Handbook (McGraw-Hill), ch. 6 (ASME and ISO compared), ch. 9 (statistical tolerancing) and ch. 24 (pinned interfaces, tutorial X); Gene R. Cogorno (above), ch. 3, 7, 8 and 13: the checks, the fastener formulas and the gauge sizes.

  • Fischer (above), ch. 15: the lever of an angle error, tutorial X.

  • Cogorno (above), ch. 12 and 13: composite position and its graphic analysis, tutorial AA. Drake (above), ch. 20 and 25: measurement systems and the gauge study example, tutorial AB; ch. 4: the title and revision blocks, tutorial AC.

  • The PLM books of John Stark, Michael Grieves, and Antti Saaksvuori and Anselmi Immonen: release and engineering change, tutorial V.

  • Tutorial U’s layout is ours; Assembly Clearance follows NX’s.

  • Tutorial AE is ours: the acceptance case of the Extrude command (None for a new body, Unite, Subtract, edit, cancel, undo, reopen).

  • Tutorial AL is ours: the plate and its parameters; the exchange follows ISO 10303-55 annex E and ISO 10303-108 Cor.1 annex F.

  • Tutorial AM is ours: the fixture and the bracket profile; the commands follow ISO 10303-112 clause 4.2.2 (selection by ISO 10303-55).

  • Tutorial BC is ours: tutorial V’s guide bush handed to manufacturing; the records are Carbon’s (Carbon’s documentation).

  • Ming C. Leu, Wenjin Tao, Amir Ghazanfari and Krishna Kolan, NX 12 for Engineering Design (Missouri University of Science and Technology), sections 3.5 and 3.6: tutorials AF, AG, AH, AJ and AK; sections 4.2.1, 4.3 and 4.5.1: tutorial AN; section 4.2.2: tutorial AP; section 4.4: tutorial AO; sections 4.7.1 and 4.7.2: tutorials AQ and AR; sections 4.7.3, 4.7.4 and 4.7.5: tutorials AS, AT and AU; exercise 4.10.1: tutorial AV; exercise 4.10.2: tutorial AW; exercise 4.10.3: tutorial AX; exercise 4.10.4: tutorial AY; sections 5.2 to 5.4: tutorial AZ; section 5.5: tutorial BA; section 5.6: tutorial BB (exercise 5.7 is not replayed).

  • ISO values (ISO 286 limits, ISO 2768 general tolerances) were checked against two published tables each; the unit tests also compare them with the ISO 286-1 formulas.
