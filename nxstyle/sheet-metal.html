Sheet Metal

Ctrl+Shift+M opens the Sheet Metal application, as in NX. Its features come from FreeCAD’s SheetMetal add-on (LGPL), which NX-Style bundles in vendor/FreeCAD_SheetMetal and the launcher loads. NX-Style gives it NX’s ribbon and names, and adds what NX keeps per part: the Sheet Metal Preferences and a Flat Pattern that uses them.

The Analysis, PMI, View, Tools, PLM and Application tabs are those of Modeling.

Sheet Metal Preferences

Material Thickness, Bend Radius and Neutral Factor for new features. They are the add-on’s own defaults, so Tab and Flange start from them, and Flat Pattern unfolds with the neutral factor (the add-on’s own Unfold starts at a fixed 0.4).

The dialog shows what the values give for a 90° bend, and checks the neutral factor:

  • the neutral factor K places the neutral layer, the one that keeps its length, at K × T from the inside of the bend. Shih gives 0.25 to 0.5 as the practical range, and his exercise uses 0.41, which is also NX-Style’s starting value; values outside 0.25 to 0.5 are flagged, values outside 0 to 0.5 refused. DIN 6935 quotes k = 2K, so a DIN value typed here is refused rather than silently doubling the bend allowance;

  • a bend radius smaller than the thickness is noted: check the material’s minimum bend radius.

Bend allowance and flat length

For a bend through the angle A (90° for a right-angle flange), inside radius R, thickness T (Shih, Parametric Modeling with Siemens NX, ch. 13):

bend allowance     BA   = A (R + K T)            A in radians
outside setback    OSSB = tan(A/2) (R + T)
bend deduction     BD   = 2 OSSB − BA

  • dimensioned between bend tangent lines: flat length = the straight lengths + BA per bend;

  • dimensioned to the outside (outer sharp to outer sharp): flat length = the outside lengths − BD per bend.

nxstyle/sheetmetal.py has both; its unit tests reproduce Shih’s hand calculation of the actuator bracket. Tutorial W builds that bracket and compares the add-on’s flat pattern with the book: 67.0523 mm against 2 × 1.31992 in = 67.0519 mm (the book rounds to five places).

Switching it off

python launch_nx.py --no-sheetmetal, or "sheet_metal": "none" in nxstyle.json, starts without the add-on; Ctrl+Shift+M then explains how to get it. An add-on already installed in the NX profile’s Mod folder is used instead of the bundled one, never both.

What differs from NX

  • NX keeps the sheet metal preferences per part; the add-on keeps them per user.

  • NX’s Flat Pattern is a feature with its own drawing view type; here the flat pattern is a solid (with sketches of its outline and bend lines) that a drawing views from above.

  • NX’s Contour Flange, Lofted Flange, Jog and Gusset have no add-on equivalent; a Tab from an open, bent profile covers the contour flange.
