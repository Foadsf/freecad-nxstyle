More ways to turn intent into geometry

Recent additions bring feature creation, sketch control and STEP exchange closer to the way you work. See what changed →

  • Pick the curves. Build the feature.
    Extrude and Revolve now accept a whole sketch or selected curves from one sketch, with tangent and connected selection rules. Their command boxes bring Boolean choice and validation into the workflow.

  • Start with a solid.
    Block, Cylinder, Cone and Sphere have NX-style dialogs, point picking, live previews and Boolean operations. Continue with datums, holes and detailed features.

  • Exchange more than a shape.
    Export and rebuild supported feature history, sketch constraints and parameters. Check incoming STEP files, identify the application protocol and inspect the product structure.

Familiar on the surface. FreeCAD at the core.

NX-Style adds the ribbon, navigators, selection tools and command workflows to FreeCAD. Some commands have NX-style dialogs; others use FreeCAD’s task panels. Your models remain .FCStd files, and your normal FreeCAD profile stays separate.

It is not Siemens NX or a claim of full NX capability. The guides explain supported options and the differences you will encounter, including limits on selected sections and STEP feature history.
