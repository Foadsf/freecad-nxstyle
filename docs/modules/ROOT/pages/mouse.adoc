= Mouse and view control
:description: NX's mouse buttons in FreeCAD: rotate, pan, zoom, OK, radial menu.

NX names the mouse buttons *MB1* (left), *MB2* (middle, or the wheel pressed) and *MB3* (right).
NX-Style gives them NX's meaning in every 3D view.

[cols="2,3",options="header"]
|===
| Mouse | Does

| MB1 click | Select. kbd:[Ctrl]+MB1 adds to or removes from the selection. When the nearest thing is refused by the filter, the click selects through to the nearest allowed one behind it.
| *Rest the pointer, then MB1 click* | *QuickPick*: over overlapping objects the cursor shows three dots; the click opens a list of everything under it, to pick one (xref:selection.adoc[]). Holding MB1 still opens it too.
| MB2 drag | Rotate the view about its centre.
| Press and hold MB2 on the model, then drag | Rotate about the point under the cursor. The cursor becomes a cross when the point is set.
| kbd:[Shift]+MB2 drag, or MB2+MB3 drag | Pan.
| kbd:[Ctrl]+MB2 drag, or MB1+MB2 drag | Zoom (dragging up zooms in). MB1 and MB2 may be pressed in either order.
| Wheel | Zoom towards the cursor.
| *MB2 click* | *OK* in the open feature dialog. On a drawing sheet it presses OK in the open drawing dialog too.
| MB2 click while a sketch tool is active | Ends the tool (the line, rectangle, circle …), as in NX.
| MB3 click | The context menu.
| *Hold MB3* | The *radial menu*: Fit, Snap View, Trimetric, Isometric and display modes over empty space; the object's own commands over a part.
|===

== View keys

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Key | View

| kbd:[Home] | Trimetric
| kbd:[End] | Isometric
| kbd:[Ctrl+Alt+F] / kbd:[Ctrl+Alt+T] / kbd:[Ctrl+Alt+R] / kbd:[Ctrl+Alt+L] | Front / Top / Right / Left
| kbd:[F8] | Snap to the closest standard view
| kbd:[Shift+F8] | Look straight at the sketch being edited
| kbd:[Ctrl+F] | Fit everything in the window
| kbd:[F6] | Zoom to a rectangle you drag
| kbd:[F7] | Rotate mode: MB1 drag rotates until kbd:[Esc], MB2 or MB3
| kbd:[F5] | Redraw the view
|===

The full list is on xref:keyboard-shortcuts.adoc[].

== Changing the feel

The xref:settings.adoc[settings] adjust the mouse:

* `rotate_degrees_per_pixel` -- how fast MB2 drag turns the view;
* `zoom_at_cursor`, `invert_wheel_zoom`, `zoom_drag_up_zooms_in` -- zoom direction and centre;
* `mb2_hold_sets_rotation_point_ms`, `mb3_hold_radial_menu_ms` -- how long a hold takes (`0` switches the radial menu off);
* `quickpick_hold_ms` -- how long the pointer rests (or MB1 is held) before QuickPick (`0` switches QuickPick and select-through off);
* `mb2_click_is_ok` -- whether an MB2 click accepts the dialog;
* `navigation` -- `"native"` uses FreeCAD 1.1's own Siemens NX navigation style instead of NX-Style's; a FreeCAD style by its menu name (`"Touchpad"`, `"Blender"`, `"Gesture"`...) uses that one.

[#orbit]
== Orbit style

MB2 drag follows FreeCAD's orbit style (*File › Preferences › Display › Navigation › Orbit style*):

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Orbit style | MB2 drag

| Trackball Classic (what NX-Style sets in a profile that has none) | NX's own rotation, about the screen's vertical and horizontal axes.
| Turntable | About the model's vertical (Z) axis or the screen's horizontal axis, whichever way the pointer mostly moves; the horizon stays level.
| Free Turntable | Both at once: about Z and the screen's horizontal axis; the horizon stays level.
| Trackball, Rounded Arcball | A trackball: dragged near the window's edge, the view rolls.
|===

FreeCAD saves the orbit style shown in Preferences whenever you press OK there, so NX-Style puts Trackball Classic in a profile that has none: Preferences then show the rotation MB2 has, and saving them for any other reason keeps it.
An orbit style already in the profile, chosen there or imported from your normal FreeCAD (xref:settings.adoc#import[]), is kept.

A change in Preferences counts from the next drag; kbd:[F7] Rotate turns the same way.
How fast the view turns is still `rotate_degrees_per_pixel`.
To keep NX's rotation whatever the orbit style says, set `"orbit_style": "nx"`.

FreeCAD 1.0 has no Trackball Classic and saves Trackball whenever its Preferences are saved, so there MB2 keeps NX's rotation; `"orbit_style": "freecad"` makes it follow the orbit style on 1.0 as well.

A navigation style picked from FreeCAD's status bar applies to that view for the session; NX-Style's own MB2 handling steps aside while the view runs it.
At the next start the view gets the style `navigation` names again.
