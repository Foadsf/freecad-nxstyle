= Assemblies
:description: Building assemblies with NX's constraint names on FreeCAD's Assembly workbench.

The *Assemblies* application is FreeCAD 1.1's Assembly workbench.
It shows the Modeling ribbon with the *Assemblies* tab in front, so modelling commands stay at hand.

image::tutorial-G-shaft-support.png[The Shaft Support assembly, cap screws in their counterbores]

== A new assembly

. *Application › Assemblies*.
. kbd:[Ctrl+N]: in the Assemblies application, a new document starts with an empty Assembly, as NX's *Assembly* template does.
. Save it (kbd:[Ctrl+S]) next to its parts; NX names the assembly in the New dialog, FreeCAD at the first save.

== Adding components

*Assembly › Add Component* lists the open parts, and its dialog opens others from disk.
Click one and press *MB2*.

* The first component asks *Ground Part?*: the same as NX's question about the Fixed constraint.
* FreeCAD places a new component beside the last one; NX lets you drag it to a spot first.
* *Create New Component* starts a new part inside the assembly.

== Constraints

FreeCAD calls constraints *joints*.
The Assembly Constraints group carries NX's names:

[cols="1,1,3",options="header"]
|===
| NX constraint | FreeCAD joint | Use

| Touch Align | Distance | Two faces against each other (offset 0), or at a distance.
| Concentric | Revolute | A hole on a pin: on one axis, faces together, free to turn.
| Align Axes | Cylindrical | Two cylinders or holes on one axis; free to slide and turn.
| Align/Lock | Slider | On one axis, free to slide, not to turn.
| Fix | Ground | Holds a component where it is.
| Bond | Fixed | Two components move as one.
| Parallel | Parallel | Two faces or edges parallel.
| Perpendicular | Perpendicular | Two faces or edges at a right angle.
| Angle | Angle | Two faces or edges at an angle.
|===

Ball, Rack and Pinion, Screw and Gear are FreeCAD joints with no NX counterpart on that group.

Select the two faces or edges, click the constraint, and press *MB2*.
One FreeCAD joint can do what two NX constraints do: a Revolute is NX's Touch Align plus the axis.

=== Degrees of freedom

NX's *Show Degrees of Freedom* has no FreeCAD equivalent.
To see whether a part can still move, drag it with MB1 in the graphics window: a part with freedom left follows the drag the way its joints allow, a fully constrained one stays.
xref:tutorials/g.adoc[Tutorial G] checks the book's degree-of-freedom results at each step this way.

TIP: FreeCAD 1.1.4 drops a *Parallel* joint whose faces start exactly perpendicular: the part does not turn although the solver reports success.
Delete the joint, turn the part a little and apply Parallel again (xref:known-issues.adoc[]).

== Editing a part

Open the part, change it and save; the assembly follows when it recomputes.
NX's *Open in Window* is opening the part's own document.

== Exploded views and parts lists

* *Exploded Views › New Explosion* moves parts by dragging a 3D handle; it has no distance field like NX's.
* *Reports › Bill of Materials* makes a spreadsheet with the parts and their quantities.
  NX puts the parts list on the drawing; a FreeCAD drawing can show the spreadsheet as a Tabular Note.
