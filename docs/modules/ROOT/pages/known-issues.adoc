= Known issues
:description: FreeCAD limits met while replaying the tutorials, with workarounds, and issues that are fixed.

These are FreeCAD 1.1.4 behaviours, observed in the UI and reproduced where noted.
NX-Style cannot change them; each has a workaround.

== FreeCAD limits

Until Next into a hole (Tutorial L)::
A subtracting extrude with End = *To first* (FreeCAD's Until Next) stops 1 mm into the part instead of at the hole's wall.
*Up to face* with the hole's own face does the same (both measured headless too).
*Workaround:* give the depth as a value; in xref:tutorials/l.adoc[Tutorial L] 25 mm, to the hole's axis.

A suppressed child of a suppressed parent (Tutorial M)::
After a parent feature is suppressed, a sketch on its face cannot find the face ("subshape not found").
Suppressing that child as well still leaves the part out of date.
*Workaround:* unsuppress the parent, or sketch the child on a datum plane so it does not depend on the parent (xref:tutorials/m.adoc[Tutorial M]).

Offset constraints on a closed loop (Tutorial I)::
With *Add offset constraint* on, Offset Curve gives a closed loop one redundant constraint; the sketch does not solve and the extrude fails.
*Workaround:* *Sketch › Select Redundant Constraints*, then Delete.

Shell after Edge Blend (Tutorial F)::
With edges rounded first, *Shell* fails ("Failed to make thick solid"); the same faces and thickness work when the shell comes first.
*Workaround:* undo, shell, then round.

Edge Blend on thin walls (Tutorial F)::
Rounding a handle's outer edges at 3.81 mm on 3.175 mm walls fails ("BRep_API: command not done"); 2.54 mm works.

Parallel from a perpendicular start (Tutorial G)::
A Parallel joint between faces that start exactly perpendicular is accepted, the solver reports success, but the part does not turn: the solver removes part of the constraint as redundant.
Reproduced in plain FreeCAD 1.1.4 with two boxes.
*Workaround:* delete the joint, turn the part a little, apply Parallel again.

Body origin and view fit::
In plain FreeCAD 1.1.4, a Body whose origin planes stay visible disappears from the 3D view after a view fit.
NX-Style therefore keeps the origin hidden; FreeCAD shows it while you pick a sketch plane.

== Fixed in NX-Style

Switching application kept the wrong part::
File › New followed by *Application › Modeling* sent the next feature into the part opened before.
Switching application now keeps the active window.

Suppress ran Freeze::
On FreeCAD 1.1.4 the ribbon's Suppress ran FreeCAD's Freeze; it is now NX-Style's own Suppress (the feature's Suppressed property).

Empty tab strip over the Top Border Bar::
Qt could leave an empty tab strip painted over *Menu* and the Type Filter after a panel moved; NX-Style hides such strips and the self-test checks for them.

MB2 rotate turning into zoom::
A stale modifier state after kbd:[Ctrl] shortcuts could turn MB2 rotate into zoom; MB2 now reads kbd:[Shift] and kbd:[Ctrl] from the mouse event.

== Reporting an issue

Open an issue on {repo}/issues[GitHub] with your FreeCAD version (*Help › About FreeCAD*, *Copy to clipboard*), what you did and what happened.
