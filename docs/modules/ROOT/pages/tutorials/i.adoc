= Tutorial I: The Gasket
:description: Extend, Quick Trim and Fillet in a sketch, a fully defined outline, and an associative 15 mm offset cut.

[horizontal]
Book:: Shih, _Parametric Modeling with Siemens NX_, ch. 6 (a millimetre part)
You learn:: Quick Extend, Quick Trim, sketch Fillet, fully defining a sketch, Project Curve, Offset Curve, a design change the offset follows
Sizes:: R20 fillets, a 5 mm plate, a 15 mm offset, the height changed to 150 mm. The outline's sizes are in figures only: an 80 mm left side and an R30 boss here.

image::tutorial-I-gasket.png[The Gasket at 150 mm height, the offset cut 15 mm inside the outline]

== In short

. An outline, a circle and a line tangent to it; kbd:[E] (Extend) and kbd:[T] (Quick Trim).
. The book's dimensions until the solver says *Fully constrained*.
. kbd:[F] (Fillet) R20; add the R20 dimensions.
. Extrude 5 mm.
. On the front face: *Project Curve*, *Offset Curve* 15 mm inwards, *Extrude (Subtract)* through all with only the inner loop.
. The design change: the overall height to 150 mm; the offset follows.

== What differs from NX

* A sketch fillet does not dimension its radius and removes the constraints that held its corner; put them back.
* Offset Curve takes the side and the distance from an MB1 click; type the exact distance into its dimension afterwards.
* With *Add offset constraint* on, FreeCAD 1.1.4 gives the closed loop one redundant constraint. *Sketch › Select Redundant Constraints*, then Delete (I10).
