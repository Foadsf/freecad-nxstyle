= Tutorial Z: MBD exchange through STEP AP242
:description: A mounting plate documented in the model, its datum reference frames checked, sent as STEP AP242 with PMI, and received into a new part with the same PMI on the same faces.

[horizontal]
Books:: Drake, _Dimensioning and Tolerancing Handbook_, 5.9.7 (degrees of freedom of a datum reference frame); the plate is ours
Standards:: ISO 10303-242 (STEP AP242: semantic PMI), ISO 5459 (datums), ISO 1101 (geometrical tolerances), ISO 286
You learn:: the Inspection Plan's datum reference frames, STEP with PMI, Import STEP with PMI, planning an inspection from the file alone
Sizes:: A plate 120 × 80 × 10 with four Ø8 holes

Model-based definition means the model carries the tolerances, and they travel with it as data.
Here they go out to a supplier in a STEP file and come back into an empty part as PMI, ready for the inspection plan.

== Step by step

=== 1. The plate and its PMI

The plate and its PMI are made by script, as tutorials xref:tutorials/t.adoc[T] and xref:tutorials/y.adoc[Y] make them with the ribbon: datums A (underside), B (left side), C (front side), the hole Ø8 H11 positioned Ø0.2 Ⓜ to A|B|C, side B perpendicular 0.05 to A, the thickness 10 ±0.1.

=== 2. The datum reference frames

*PMI › Inspection Plan*. Below the characteristics:

----
A | B | C arrests 6 of 6 degrees of freedom (A 3, B 2, C 1)
A arrests 3 of 6 degrees of freedom (A 3); free: translation along X; translation along Y; rotation about Z
----

The position needs all six; the perpendicularity of B needs only A.

image::tutorial-Z-01-frames.png[The datum reference frames in the Inspection Plan]

=== 3. Out: STEP with PMI

*PMI › STEP with PMI*, a file name, OK: datums A, B and C, two datum systems, the size with H11 and the thickness with ±0.1, the position and the perpendicularity.

=== 4. In: Import STEP with PMI

kbd:[Ctrl+N], *PMI › Import STEP with PMI*, the file, OK.
The plate comes in with seven PMI, the same as were written, on the same faces.

image::tutorial-Z-02-import.png[What came in: the part and its seven PMI]

=== 5. The supplier's inspection plan

*Inspection Plan* on the imported part: the same characteristics, limits and datum reference frames as at the source.

== What differs from NX

* NX exchanges PMI through STEP AP242 and JT, with graphical PMI and saved views; NX-Style's files carry the semantic PMI only.
* A count (4X), the envelope Ⓔ and the ISO 2768 note have no AP242 form in OpenCASCADE, so this plate is dimensioned without them.
* The report of the degrees of freedom a datum system arrests is NX-Style's.
* The file carries the shape and its PMI; xref:tutorials/al.adoc[tutorial AL] sends the history, the sketch constraints and the parameters too.
