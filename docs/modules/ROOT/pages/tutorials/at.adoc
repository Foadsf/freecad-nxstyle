= Tutorial AT: Arbor Press Rack
:description: A rack: a sketched rectangle extruded, a tapered tooth space cut with Extrude (Subtract), patterned 19 times, and a hole at the centre of the end face found with Between Two Points (NX 12 for Engineering Design, 4.7.4).

[horizontal]
Source:: Ming C. Leu, Wenjin Tao, Amir Ghazanfari and Krishna Kolan, _NX 12 for Engineering Design_ (Missouri University of Science and Technology), section 4.7.4 (Rack).
You learn:: Rectangle, Extrude, a tapered cut with Extrude (Subtract), Pattern Feature (Linear, Count and Pitch), the Point Dialog's Between Two Points
Sizes:: the book's, in inches, typed in millimetres. A rectangle from (0, 0) to (240, 25), extruded 20. A tooth space 35 long across the bar, 6 wide and 5.034 deep, its walls tapered 14.5°, its centre lines 37.8 from the end and 10 from the side. 19 of them at a pitch of 9.4. A hole Ø10, 20 deep, into the far end.

The book gives the pocket's sizes only in its figure (p. 89); they are listed above.

== Step by step

=== 1. The bar

kbd:[Ctrl+N] and save the part as `Arborpress_rack`. kbd:[End] turns the view to Isometric.
Click *Sketch*, pick the XC-YC plane and press MB2.
Press kbd:[R] for *Rectangle*.
Type the first corner (0, 0) in the fields beside the pointer, then the opposite corner (6096, 635) mm, which is (240, 25) in.
Press MB2, then kbd:[Ctrl+Q].

Click *Extrude*, click a side of the rectangle, and type 508 mm (20 in).
Click *OK*.

=== 2. One tooth space

Select the bar's top face and click *Sketch*.
Draw a *Rectangle* from (34.8, −7.5) to (40.8, 27.5) in, typed in mm.
That is the book's pocket: 6 wide along XC and 35 long across the bar, centred 37.8 from the end and 10 from the side. It runs out over both sides of the bar.
Press MB2, then kbd:[Ctrl+Q].

Click *Extrude (Subtract)*, click a side of the rectangle (QuickPick lists it where it lies on the face), type the length 127.8636 mm (5.034 in) and the taper −14.5°, and click *OK*.

=== 3. The teeth

Select the Extrude (Subtract) and click *Pattern Feature*.
Choose the X axis as Direction 1, *Spacing*, 19 occurrences and 238.76 mm (9.4 in).
Press MB2.

=== 4. The hole

Click *Cylinder* and choose *−XC*.
In Specify Point choose *Between Two Points* and click two opposite corners of the far end face: the point is the face's centre.
Type Diameter 254 and Height 508 mm (10 and 20 in), choose *Subtract*, and click *OK*.

== What differs from NX

* NX 12 draws the rectangle with Insert > Curve > Rectangle, a curve outside sketches. Here, as in NX 2212, it is drawn in a sketch.
* NX 12's Rectangular Pocket is a legacy feature, positioned with Perpendicular distances to two edges. NX 2212 cuts it with Extrude (Subtract) from a sketched rectangle; its corners come from the pocket's size and position.
* FreeCAD's taper is negative for walls that close in with depth. NX's pocket's taper of 14.5° is −14.5° here.
