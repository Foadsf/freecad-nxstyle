== Step by step

=== 1. Sheet and views

Open `Impeller_hexa-bolt`, press kbd:[Ctrl+Shift+D] and click *New Sheet*.
Choose the template `ANSIE_Landscape.svg` (E, 34 x 44 in), the scale 8 and *Third angle*.

Select the screw's bodies and click *Base View*, direction Front: the screw stands with its head up.
Select the view, click *Projected View*, then *Right* and *Above*, and press MB2.
The view above is the top view: the hexagon.
Click *Display Borders* to hide the borders.

Select the three views and turn on their *Hard Hidden*: hidden lines are drawn dashed.
The top view now shows the shank under the head as a dashed circle.

=== 2. Dimensions

Pick two vertices and click *Vertical*:

[cols="1,3,1",options="header"]
|===
| View | Length | Book (in)
| Front | the whole screw, from its end to the top of the head | 1.703
| Front | the head | 0.188
| Front | from the top of the head down to where the sphere meets the hexagon's corners | 0.065
| Right | the step under the head | 0.016
|===

=== 3. The thread note

Select an edge of the thread on the front view and click *Note*.
Type `Right Hand`, click the *Ø* button, type `0.20 x 1.5`, press kbd:[Enter], type `Pitch 0.05, Angle 60` and click the *°* button.
Set the lettering height to 7 mm and the stub length to 12.7 mm, and press MB2.
The leader runs from the thread to the note.

=== 4. Radial and the isometric view

Select the circle on top of the head in the top view and click *Radial*.
Select the screw's bodies again, click *Base View*, and set the direction to isometric.

The views' directions are checked, and the top view must show a hidden circle of the shank's radius.
Each dimension is compared with the screw's own geometry: its height, the head, the step, and where the head's sphere meets the hexagon.
It must also match the book's number to its three decimals.
The note's arrow is traced back from the sheet into the model and must end on the threaded shank.

== What differs from NX

* As in xref:tutorials/az.adoc[tutorial AZ], the sheet's size, scale and projection are properties, and a view's direction is a property.
* NX sets the hidden line style in the views' Settings dialog. TechDraw's *Hard Hidden* draws hidden lines dashed.
* NX drags the leader from the shaft and sets the lettering height and leader length in Settings afterwards. Here they are fields of the Note dialog.
* The note's text is the book's. It gives Ø0.20, while AQ's thread is the book's 1/4-20 UNC on a Ø0.25 shank.
* The book's figure labels the circle on top of the head Ø0.200, which is the size of its radius (0.201). TechDraw's *Radial* shows it as a radius.
* TechDraw computes hidden lines for the detailed thread, so these views take longer to appear than plain ones.
