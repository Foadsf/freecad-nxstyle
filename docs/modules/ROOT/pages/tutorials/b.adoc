= Tutorial B: First part, cylinder and cone

You learn:: Circle, Extrude, Revolve about a sketch axis
Sizes:: Hollow cylinder Ø50/Ø30 × 50 mm; cone R25 × 50 mm

image::tutorial-B-first-part.png[Cylinder and cone]

== In short

. kbd:[Ctrl+N], kbd:[X], *XY_Plane*, *MB2*.
. kbd:[O] twice for the two circles, kbd:[D] for Ø50 and Ø30, kbd:[Ctrl+Q], 50 mm, *MB2*.
. A new part; *Revolve* with nothing selected; a triangle of R25 × 50 mm with kbd:[Z]; kbd:[Ctrl+Q]; revolve about the sketch's vertical axis, *MB2*.
