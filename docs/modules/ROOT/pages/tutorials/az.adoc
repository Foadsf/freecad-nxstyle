= Tutorial AZ: Arbor Press Rack Drawing
:description: The arbor press rack of tutorial AT on a B sheet at 1:25: a front base view, top and right projected views, borders off, dimensions on all three views and a section through the hole (NX 12 for Engineering Design, 5.2 to 5.4).

[horizontal]
Source:: Ming C. Leu, Wenjin Tao, Amir Ghazanfari and Krishna Kolan, _NX 12 for Engineering Design_ (Missouri University of Science and Technology), sections 5.2 (creating a drawing), 5.3 (dimensioning) and 5.4 (sectional view).
You learn:: New Sheet (size, scale, projection), Base View, Projected View, dimensions, Section View
Part:: tutorial xref:tutorials/at.adoc[AT]'s rack, `Arborpress_rack`. The replay runs AT first.
Sizes:: the book's, in inches, typed in millimetres: the drawing's dimensions read 25.4 times the book's numbers.

== Step by step

=== 1. The sheet

Open `Arborpress_rack` and press kbd:[Ctrl+Shift+D] for Drafting.
Click *New Sheet*.
In the sheet's properties, choose the template `ANSIB_Landscape.svg` (B, 11 x 17 in), the scale 0.04 (1:25) and *Third angle*.

=== 2. The views

Select the rack's body and click *Base View*.
Set the view's direction to Front and press MB2 to place it.

Select the front view and click *Projected View*.
Click *Above* and *Right*, then press MB2.
The top view and the right side view appear lined up with the front view, at its scale.
The right view shows the hole in the end of the rack as a circle.

The book now turns off Display Borders. A FreeCAD sheet starts with *Show Frames* off, so there is nothing to press.

=== 3. Dimensions

Pick the two end vertices of each length in the view and click *Horizontal* or *Vertical*:

[cols="1,2,1,1",options="header"]
|===
| View | Length | Book (in) | Drawing (mm)
| Top | the rack's length | 240 | 6096
| Top | from the left end to the first tooth space | 34.8 | 883.92
| Front | the tooth space's opening | 6.000 | 152.4
| Front | its floor | 3.396 | 86.26
| Front | its depth | 5.034 | 127.86
| Right | the height | 20 | 508
| Right | the width | 25 | 635
|===

Drag each dimension clear of its view.
Where a point has no vertex (the foot of a tooth space), the replay puts a cosmetic vertex on the edge there first.

=== 4. The section

Select the front view and click *Section View*.
Click the *Up* direction and press MB2.
TechDraw cuts through the middle of the view, at the height of the hole's axis.
Move the section below the front view.

image::tutorial-AZ-01-drawing.png[The rack's drawing: top, front and right views with their dimensions, and section A-A through the hole]

Save the part with kbd:[Ctrl+S]; the drawing is saved in it.

Every check reads the drawing back from TechDraw and compares it with the model.
The views' edges must span what the Part API gives for each direction.
Each dimension must equal the length the Part API's tooth space and bar give, and the book's number times 25.4.
The section must show the hole's walls one diameter apart, which happens only when the cut goes through the hole's axis.

== What differs from NX

* NX asks for the sheet size, scale and projection in one Sheet dialog. In FreeCAD they are the sheet's properties.
* NX lists the model views in the Base View dialog. A TechDraw view takes its direction as a property.
* NX places a projected view where you click round the parent. Here you click a side button. In NX a projected view follows its parent when the parent is dragged; here it stays where it was placed.
* NX shows a border round each view until Display Borders is unchecked. A FreeCAD sheet starts with *Show Frames* off, and FreeCAD's *Turn View Frames On/Off* stays greyed in 1.1.4. The replay reports the property; it did not measure how the sheet looks.
* NX infers horizontal or vertical from where you move the pointer. Here you choose the *Horizontal* or *Vertical* button.
* TechDraw dimensions attach to vertices. Where NX snaps to any point on an edge, the replay adds a cosmetic vertex first.
* The book's text puts the 240 on the front view, but its figures put it on the top view. The replay follows the figures.
* TechDraw puts a new section view on top of its base view. You move it afterwards.
