= Tutorial Y: PMI in model views
:description: NX's model views for PMI: the work view, PMI that belong to a view and lie in its plane, switching views, and Display in Views.

[horizontal]
Book:: The model views of Wünsch, Pilz and Vajna, _Siemens NX für Einsteiger – kurz und bündig_, 4th ed., section 6.10, and Sham Tickoo, _Siemens NX 12.0 for Designers_, ch. 12; the plate is ours
You learn:: the Model Views folder, the work view (double-click), PMI in a view and in its plane, Display in Views, Show All PMI
Sizes:: A plate 80 × 60 × 10 with four Ø8 holes

A part is documented in more than one direction: its hole pattern is read from above, its thickness and flatness from the front.
NX keeps each in its own model view, so neither view is cluttered by the other's annotations.

== Step by step

=== 1. The plate and its model views

The plate is made by script. *PMI › Tolerancing*, OK: the part gets its PMI folder and a *Model Views* folder with Front, Top, Right, Back, Bottom, Left, Isometric and Trimetric.

=== 2. The Top view

Double-click *Top* under Model Views: it becomes the work view, and the camera looks down on the plate.
Select a hole, *Rapid Dimension*, class `H11`, quantity 4: _4X Ø8.000 H11_.
Select the front and back faces, *Rapid Dimension*: the width 60, covered by the general tolerance.
Both go into Top, their labels in its plane.

=== 3. The Front view

Double-click *Front*: Top's two PMI disappear.
*Rapid Dimension* on the underside and the top face, ±0.1: the thickness.
*Datum Feature Symbol* A on the underside, *Feature Control Frame* flatness 0.05 on the top.

image::tutorial-Y-02-front.png[The Front model view: thickness, datum A and flatness]

=== 4. Back to Top

Double-click *Top*: only the hole size and the width show.

image::tutorial-Y-01-top.png[The Top model view: the hole size and the width]

=== 5. Display in Views

Datum A is referenced from above too. Select it, *PMI › Display in Views*, tick *Top* beside Front, OK: it now shows in both.

=== 6. Isometric, and Show All PMI

Double-click *Isometric*: it has no PMI of its own, so nothing shows.
*Show All PMI* ends the work view; all five show, each in its own view's plane.

== What differs from NX

* NX's new part has its model views from the start; here they come with the part's first PMI.
* The replay double-clicks a model view by calling the handler the Part Navigator calls.
* *Display in Views* is on the PMI tab here, on the PMI's right-click menu in NX. *Show All PMI* is NX-Style's.
