= Tutorial E: Saddle Bracket
:description: The BORN technique, a symmetric extrude, renamed features, three design changes, a material and the mass.

[horizontal]
Book:: Shih, _Parametric Modeling with Siemens NX_, ch. 4
You learn:: Datum CSYS first (BORN), Symmetric extrude, Project Curve, renaming in the Part Navigator, Edit Parameters, Assign Material, Measure Bodies
Sizes:: The text gives 63.5, 12.7, 50.8, Ø19.05 and R6.35 mm (2.5, 0.5, 2.0, Ø0.75 and R0.25 in). The L profile is in figures only, so it is this project's own: 76.2 mm long, a 12.7 mm base, a 19.05 × 50.8 mm upright, a 25.4 × 19.05 mm notch.

image::tutorial-E-saddle-bracket.png[The Saddle Bracket after the design changes]

== In short

. *Datum CSYS* first; FreeCAD asks whether to continue without an attachment; a Body has its own origin anyway.
. The L profile, extruded *Symmetric* 63.5 mm (the value is the total width, as in NX since NX 1926).
. A circular end tied to a projected edge.
. kbd:[F2] in the Part Navigator to name the features.
. Three design changes: the width 63.5 → 50.8 mm, Until Next → Through All, and a fillet added to the first sketch.
. *Render › Material › Assign Material*: steel. *Analysis › Measure › Measure Bodies*: 86,504.5 mm³, 7900 kg/m³, 0.6834 kg.

== What differs from NX

* Design changes reopen the feature's dialog (Edit Parameters) instead of editing a dimension shown in the graphics window.
* Assign Material applies at once and closes with *Close*; FreeCAD 1.1 also gives the body the material's colour.
* FreeCAD's own Measure has no mass; *Measure Bodies* is NX-Style's.
