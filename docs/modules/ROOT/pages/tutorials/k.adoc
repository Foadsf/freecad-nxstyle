= Tutorial K: Parameter-driven flat bar
:description: A bar with N equally spaced holes, driven by a table of expressions, then switched to a second configuration.

[horizontal]
Book:: Leake and Borgerson, _Engineering Design Graphics_, ch. 7, Fig. 7-30
You learn:: The Expressions table (kbd:[Ctrl+E]), dimensions tied to names, configurations
Sizes:: L, N and D for two configurations (A: 1219.2 mm, 5 holes, 50.8 mm; B: 914.4 mm, 8 holes, 25.4 mm; the book's 48, 5, 2 and 36, 8, 1 in). Width, thickness and hole size are not given: 50.8 × 12.7 mm and Ø12.7 here.

image::tutorial-K-flat-bar.png[The flat bar in configuration B, eight holes]

== In short

. kbd:[Ctrl+E]: type `L`, `N_holes`, `D` and `hole_spacing = (L - 2*D) / (N_holes - 1)` into the Expressions sheet.
. Sketch the bar; its length is `=Expressions.L`.
. The first hole at `D` from the end; *Pattern Feature* with `N_holes` occurrences, `hole_spacing` apart.
. Type configuration B's values into the same sheet: the bar follows.

== What differs from NX

* NX lists expressions in a dialog; FreeCAD keeps them in a spreadsheet. A dimension refers to a cell as `=Expressions.L`.
* FreeCAD refuses names that are unit symbols: the book's `N` (the newton) becomes `N_holes`.
