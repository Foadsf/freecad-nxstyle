= Laser Cut
:description: Flat panels joined by tabs and slots, cross halvings and living hinges, then laid flat for the laser cutter, on the LCInterlocking add-on.

*Application › Laser Cut* opens an application for parts cut from sheet, such as plywood, MDF or acrylic, on a laser cutter or a CNC router.
NX has no such application. Its features come from https://github.com/execuc/LCInterlocking[LCInterlocking] (execuc, LGPL-2.1), which NX-Style bundles in `vendor/LCInterlocking` and the launcher loads.
NX-Style gives it a ribbon with NX-style names. The add-on's own command name is shown in each button's tooltip (*FreeCAD: Slots* under *Tabs and Slots*).

== The Home tab

[cols="1,2,3",options="header"]
|===
| Button | Add-on command | Does
| *Sketch*, *Extrude*, *Block* | Sketcher, Part Extrude, Part Box | The panels: a sketched outline extruded by the sheet's thickness, or a block.
| *Panel Box* | Box | The six panels of a box (four sides, top and bottom) from its sizes and the thickness, not yet joined.
| *Rounded Panel Box* | Rounded box | The panels of a many-sided box, ready for living hinges at its corners.
| *Tabs and Slots* | Slots | Joins panels that meet: a face of one panel gets tabs, and the panel it touches gets the slots. Each joint's type is *Tab*, *Screw* (a T-slot for a screw and nut) or *Continuous* (finger joints along the whole edge). It sets the number, width and position of the tabs, dog bones and the kerf.
| *Cross Halving* | Crosspiece | Two panels that cross each other, each slotted halfway (egg-crate joints).
| *Living Hinge* | Living hinges | Joins panels round a curved corner into one flat part with a pattern of cuts there, so the sheet bends.
| *Export Flat Parts* | Export | A new document with the selected parts laid flat side by side, and their outlines as 2D views. Export these with *File › Export* as (flattened) SVG or DXF.
|===

The *Analysis*, *View*, *Tools*, *PLM* and *Application* tabs are those of Modeling.

== How a joint is made

. Model each panel as its own solid, with the sheet's thickness. Panels that meet must touch: one panel's face lies on the other's.
. *Tabs and Slots*. In the task panel, select the panels and click *Add parts*. The add-on finds each panel's thickness from its two largest faces.
. Select the faces that carry the tabs, choose the joint type, and click *Add faces*. Select a joint in the list to set its tabs. *Preview* shows the result in a separate document.
. OK, or MB2. The joined parts are new solids (`<panel>_tab`). The original panels are kept, hidden, in a group, and *Edit* on the MultiJoin brings the panel back.
. Select the joined parts, *Export Flat Parts*, then *File › Export* the outlines.

Each tab comes out wider than its slot by the kerf (*Laser beam diameter*, thickness ÷ 15 by default), so the parts press-fit once the laser has burnt that much away.
xref:tutorials/ad.adoc[Tutorial AD] joins a five-panel tray this way, checks every joined part, and exports the outlines.

== Switching it off

`python launch_nx.py --no-lcinterlocking`, or `"laser_cut": "none"` in `nxstyle.json`, starts without the add-on, and Laser Cut is then not offered.
An LCInterlocking already installed in the NX profile's `Mod` folder is used instead of the bundled one, never both.

== Limits

* Make the panels as Part solids (*Block*, *Extrude* from the Part tools, imported STEP). The add-on ignores a PartDesign Body's own placement, so a moved Body is joined in the wrong place (https://github.com/execuc/LCInterlocking/issues/38[issue 38]).
* On FreeCAD 1.1 (Qt 6), opening the *Tabs and Slots* or *Cross Halving* task panel prints a `TypeError` about `StandardButton` in the Report view. The panel still gets its OK and Cancel buttons and works (https://github.com/execuc/LCInterlocking/issues/78[issue 78]).
* On FreeCAD 1.1, *Add parts* or *Add faces* with nothing selected prints a `TypeError` instead of doing nothing (https://github.com/execuc/LCInterlocking/issues/75[issue 75]).
* The add-on's icons are 16-pixel bitmaps, so its ribbon buttons are small ones.
