= Sheet Metal
:description: NX Sheet Metal on FreeCAD's SheetMetal add-on: tabs, flanges, bends, hems, cutouts and flat patterns, with the neutral factor, bend allowance and bend deduction worked out.

kbd:[Ctrl+Shift+M] opens the *Sheet Metal* application, as in NX.
Its features come from FreeCAD's https://github.com/shaise/FreeCAD_SheetMetal[SheetMetal add-on] (LGPL), which NX-Style bundles in `vendor/FreeCAD_SheetMetal` and the launcher loads.
NX-Style gives it NX's ribbon and names, and adds what NX keeps per part: the *Sheet Metal Preferences* and a *Flat Pattern* that uses them.

== The Home tab

[cols="1,2,3",options="header"]
|===
| NX | Add-on command | Does
| *Tab* | Make Base Wall | A plate of the material thickness from a closed sketch (or a bent profile from an open one).
| *Flange* | Make Wall | A flange on selected edges: length, angle, bend radius, *Length Spec* (Leg, Outer Sharp, Inner Sharp, Tangential) and *Bend Type* (Material Inside or Outside, Thickness Outside, Offset).
| *Bend* | Fold a Wall | Bends a flat sheet along a sketched line.
| *Hem Flange* | Make Hem | A hem on an edge.
| *Extend Face*, *Extend by Sketch*, *Base Shape* | Extend Face, Extend by Sketch, Add base shape | Lengthen a wall; a ready-made bracket or channel.
| *Convert to Sheet Metal* | Convert to Sheet Metal | Turns a shelled solid into sheet metal.
| *Rip*, *Bend on Edge* | Make Junction, Make Bend | Splits a corner; adds a bend between two walls of a converted solid.
| *Bend Relief*, *Corner Relief* | Make Relief, Add Corner Relief | Reliefs at bends and corners.
| *Normal Cutout* | Extruded Cutout | A cutout through the sheet.
| *Wrapped Cutout*, *Form Feature* | Wrap Cutout, Make Forming in Wall | A sketch's cutout wrapped onto bent sheet; a formed (stamped) feature.
| *Flat Pattern* | Unfold (NX-Style's) | The flat pattern, with the preferred neutral factor.
| *Sheet Metal Preferences* | NX-Style's | Thickness, bend radius, neutral factor.
|===

The *Analysis*, *PMI*, *View*, *Tools*, *PLM* and *Application* tabs are those of Modeling.

== Sheet Metal Preferences

*Material Thickness*, *Bend Radius* and *Neutral Factor* for new features.
They are the add-on's own defaults, so *Tab* and *Flange* start from them, and *Flat Pattern* unfolds with the neutral factor (the add-on's own Unfold starts at a fixed 0.4).

The dialog shows what the values give for a 90° bend, and checks the neutral factor:

* the *neutral factor* K places the neutral layer, the one that keeps its length, at K × T from the inside of the bend. Shih gives 0.25 to 0.5 as the practical range, and his exercise uses 0.41, which is also NX-Style's starting value; values outside 0.25 to 0.5 are flagged, values outside 0 to 0.5 refused. DIN 6935 quotes k = 2K, so a DIN value typed here is refused rather than silently doubling the bend allowance;
* a bend radius smaller than the thickness is noted: check the material's minimum bend radius.

== Bend allowance and flat length

For a bend through the angle A (90° for a right-angle flange), inside radius R, thickness T (Shih, _Parametric Modeling with Siemens NX_, ch. 13):

....
bend allowance     BA   = A (R + K T)            A in radians
outside setback    OSSB = tan(A/2) (R + T)
bend deduction     BD   = 2 OSSB − BA
....

* dimensioned between bend tangent lines: flat length = the straight lengths + BA per bend;
* dimensioned to the outside (outer sharp to outer sharp): flat length = the outside lengths − BD per bend.

`nxstyle/sheetmetal.py` has both; its unit tests reproduce Shih's hand calculation of the actuator bracket.
xref:tutorials/w.adoc[Tutorial W] builds that bracket and compares the add-on's flat pattern with the book: 67.0523 mm against 2 × 1.31992 in = 67.0519 mm (the book rounds to five places).

== Switching it off

`python launch_nx.py --no-sheetmetal`, or `"sheet_metal": "none"` in `nxstyle.json`, starts without the add-on; kbd:[Ctrl+Shift+M] then explains how to get it.
An add-on already installed in the NX profile's `Mod` folder is used instead of the bundled one, never both.

== What differs from NX

* NX keeps the sheet metal preferences per part; the add-on keeps them per user.
* NX's Flat Pattern is a feature with its own drawing view type; here the flat pattern is a solid (with sketches of its outline and bend lines) that a drawing views from above.
* NX's Contour Flange, Lofted Flange, Jog and Gusset have no add-on equivalent; a Tab from an open, bent profile covers the contour flange.
