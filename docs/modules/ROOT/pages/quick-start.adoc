= Your first part in five minutes
:description: Sketch a rectangle, extrude it and cut a slot, the NX way.

This page builds a small block with a slot, using NX's keys and mouse buttons.
Every step here is one that the tutorial replay presses and checks (xref:tutorials/a.adoc[Tutorial A]).

Start NX-Style first (xref:installation.adoc[]).

== 1. A new part

Press kbd:[Ctrl+N].

NX-Style's New works like NX's *Model* template: the new part already has a Body, ready to sketch on.
Units are millimetres.

== 2. Extrude, starting from a sketch

With nothing selected, press kbd:[X] (Extrude).

The *Dialog* panel on the left asks for a sketch plane.
Click *XY_Plane* in its list (or click the plane in the graphics window) and press *MB2* (the middle mouse button) for OK.
The Sketch task environment opens, with the contextual *Sketch* tab on the ribbon.

== 3. Draw a rectangle

. Press kbd:[R] (Rectangle).
. Click two opposite corners with *MB1* in the graphics window.
. Press *MB2* to end the Rectangle tool.

== 4. Give it sizes

Press kbd:[D] (Rapid Dimension), click an edge, type a value and press kbd:[Enter].
Do the same for the other side.

NOTE: FreeCAD's dimension tool asks for one value at a time.
NX's dynamic dimensions while you draw, and its "scale the whole sketch?" prompt, do not exist here.

== 5. Finish the sketch: straight into Extrude

Press kbd:[Ctrl+Q] (Finish Sketch).

As in NX, finishing the sketch carries straight on into the Extrude dialog.
Type the distance into the length field and press *MB2* for OK.

== 6. Cut a slot into a face

. Click the face to cut into.
. On the ribbon, click *Home › Feature › Extrude (Subtract)*.
  The sketch opens on that face.
. kbd:[R], two clicks, *MB2*; give it its sizes with kbd:[D].
. kbd:[Ctrl+Q], type the depth, *MB2*.

TIP: FreeCAD's Extrude has no Boolean option: the subtracting extrude is its own button, *Extrude (Subtract)*, and it cuts into the part without a Reverse Direction.

== 7. Look at it and save

* Drag with *MB2* to rotate, *Shift+MB2* to pan, roll the wheel to zoom (xref:mouse.adoc[]).
* kbd:[Home] gives the trimetric view, kbd:[End] the isometric one, kbd:[Ctrl+F] fits the part in the window.
* kbd:[Ctrl+S] saves. The file is a FreeCAD `.FCStd` file.
