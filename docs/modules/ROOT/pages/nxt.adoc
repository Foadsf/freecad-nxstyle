= FreeCAD-Nxt (trial)
:description: The FreeCAD-Nxt add-on next to NX-Style: what it adds, how to turn it on, and where it meets NX's keys and layout.
:nxt: https://github.com/obelisk79/FreeCAD-Nxt

{nxt}[FreeCAD-Nxt] is a FreeCAD add-on by Joe (obelisk79), in the style of Fusion and Onshape and built on Qt Quick.
It adds a model panel with a rollback bar, a Property Inspector, drag-handle value boxes, its own fillet and chamfer, a section view with filled cut faces, Isolate and Quick Mass.
NX-Style bundles it, unmodified, as a git submodule (`vendor/FreeCAD-Nxt`, version 0.5.15), and it is *off by default*.
This is the first of several steps that bring parts of it into NX's commands; for now it runs beside NX-Style as it is.

image::nxt-section.png[Nxt's section view in NX-Style: the cut faces are hatched, the plane has an Offset box]

== Turning it on

* `python launch_nx.py --nxt` starts with it once.
* `"nxt": true` in the xref:settings.adoc[settings] switches it on for good.

It needs FreeCAD 1.1 or newer; on FreeCAD 1.0 the launcher says so and starts without it.
Its commands are under *Menu › View › FreeCAD-Nxt*, and its panel is the *Model (Nxt)* tab beside the Part Navigator.
A copy of Nxt installed in the NX profile's own `Mod` folder (through the Addon Manager) is used instead of the bundled one, so it never loads twice; FreeCAD loads that copy whether or not `--nxt` is given.

Nxt is not a workbench: it is a `freecad.nxt` package that adds its panel and commands to every workbench.

=== What NX-Style changes in it

Nothing in Nxt's code.
In the NX profile, NX-Style sets one of Nxt's own preferences before Nxt starts: *CheckAddonUpdates* off.
Nxt otherwise looks for updates of every installed add-on after start-up and offers the Addon Manager's _Update all_ dialog; NX-Style's add-ons are pinned versions, updated with NX-Style itself.

== What works on FreeCAD 1.1.4

Measured with the self-test (`--smoke-test --nxt`) on FreeCAD 1.1.4 (Qt 6.8.3), Windows 11.

[cols="2,1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Nxt feature | FreeCAD 1.1.4 | What was seen

| Model panel (tree, timeline) | works | Opens as the *Model (Nxt)* tab in the left tab strip; the Part Navigator stays in front.
| Property Inspector | works | Opens beside the panel for the selected feature.
| Isolate | works | Shows only the selection; leaving it shows everything again.
| Section view, filled cut faces | works | The cut faces are hatched; the plane can be dragged, with an Offset box.
| Quick Mass | works | The mass bar appears at the bottom of the graphics window.
| Drag handles with value boxes | works | Editing a Pad shows FreeCAD's two arrows and two taper handles, with Nxt's *Length* and *Taper* boxes beside them.
| Nxt fillet and chamfer | works | Opens its task panel and makes an `NxtFillet` feature that recomputes cleanly.
| Rollback (the Body's tip) | works | Nxt's panel moves the tip back to the Sketch and forward to the Pad.
| Menu entry | works | *Menu › View › FreeCAD-Nxt* lists Nxt's commands.
|===

image::nxt-pad-handles.png[A Pad in edit with Nxt on: FreeCAD's drag handles, with Nxt's Length and Taper boxes]

Nxt's drag handles are FreeCAD's own ("gizmos"), which FreeCAD 1.1 already has; nothing above needed a FreeCAD 26.x build.
Nxt's author works on FreeCAD 26.3 (Qt 6.11); its context menu uses a Qt 6.8 feature, which FreeCAD 1.1.4 ships.
Not exercised yet: drag reorder, dependency arrows, the detail strip, face snapping and the context menu, which need real mouse work in Nxt's Qt Quick panel; they come with the next steps.

== The tutorials with Nxt on

Nxt is not ready to be on by default: with it on, NX-Style's own Extrude and Revolve dialogs lose what is typed into them.
All 48 tutorial replays were run with Nxt off and on, each group of tutorials in a fresh FreeCAD 1.1.4.

[cols="1,1,1,1",options="header"]
|===
| | Steps run | FAILED | Steps not run after a failure

| Nxt off | 771 | 6 | 0
| Nxt on | 742 | 25 | 40
|===

The six with Nxt off fail with Nxt on too, the same way, and have nothing to do with Nxt (three Ctrl+S save checks, an Orient View, and two steps of the Carbon tutorial that tutorial AN runs).
The regressions with Nxt on:

* *Extrude and Revolve values are lost* (tutorials AE, AF, AJ: an End of 20 or 60 mm gives FreeCAD's default 10 mm; AO: Revolve takes none of the 10 picked curves).
  Nxt replaces FreeCAD's Pad, Pocket and Revolve parameter boxes with its own cards; with that replacement removed, tutorial AE replays as with Nxt off, while switching off only Nxt's floating value boxes does not help.
  Revolve's box is replaced the same way; that AO fails for the same reason is likely but was not tested on its own.
  Nxt has no setting for the replacement yet.
* *Offset Curve in a sketch* (tutorial I): the _Add offset constraint_ option does not take, so the offset loop stays where the click put it (14.6 mm instead of 15). It repeats; the cause is not yet known.
* *Datum Plane, Datum Axis and Hole* (tutorial AN): a typed offset reads 0, the datum axis stays unattached, and the Hole's sketch does not start. The cause is not yet known.

The other tutorials (A to H, J to Z, AA to AD, AG, AH, AK, AP to AY) replay as with Nxt off.

== Where it meets NX-Style

Measured with the self-test, Nxt on against Nxt off:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Where | What happens with Nxt on

| kbd:[Space] in the graphics window
| Nxt takes it: it hides or shows the _feature_ behind the pick (a Pad, not its Body).
FreeCAD's own Space command (Show/Hide, `Std_ToggleVisibility`) no longer gets the key.

| kbd:[Home], kbd:[End] in Nxt's panel
| While Nxt's panel has the keyboard focus, Nxt keeps them for its own list; they do not turn the view to Trimetric and Isometric.
In the graphics window they keep their NX meaning.

| kbd:[F2]
| In the Part Navigator it still opens the name editor.

| Toolbars
| Nxt adds a toolbar of its own (*Nxt Fillet/Chamfer*) when Part Design starts. It shows above the ribbon, where NX-Style hides FreeCAD's toolbars.

| Model panel
| Nxt tabs its panel beside FreeCAD's Model dock, so the Part Navigator, Dialog and Versions strip gains a *Model (Nxt)* tab.

| Colours
| Nxt takes its colours from the window around it: background `#f0f0f0`, text `#1e2329`, but a grey accent (`#606060`) where NX-Style uses teal (`#0B6E79`).

| Drag-handle preferences
| On its first run Nxt sets two FreeCAD preferences that no FreeCAD page shows: a plain drag moves in fine steps and kbd:[Ctrl] gives coarse ones (FreeCAD: coarse first, kbd:[Shift] for fine).
Nxt records what it replaced and puts it back when it is disabled or removed.

| Sketch profiles
| Nxt's sketch repair is on, and changes the sketch without asking: a Pad on a rectangle with a 0.01 mm gap, which fails with Nxt off, is valid with Nxt on, because Nxt added a Coincident constraint. It offers Undo afterwards.

| Task dialogs
| Nxt replaces the parameter boxes of FreeCAD's Pad, Pocket and Revolve dialogs, which NX-Style's Extrude and Revolve drive (see the tutorials above).
|===

== Turning it off again

Leave out `--nxt`, or set `"nxt": false` (the default).
Nxt's own settings stay in the NX profile's `user.cfg` under `Mod/Nxt`; they do nothing while Nxt is off.
